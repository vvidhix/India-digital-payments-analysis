# India-digital-payments-analysis/ RBI data 2019- 2025 
Overview
Analysis of India's digital payment ecosystem using Reserve Bank of India 
monthly payment systems data spanning 2019 to 2025.

 Data Source
Reserve Bank of India — Database on Indian Economy (DBIE)
Sourced via Kaggle | 73 months of data

Tools Used
- SQL (MySQL) — data analysis and querying
- Notion — project documentation and findings with powerbi dashboards. 

 Key Findings
1. October 2025 recorded peak UPI volume at 207,009 lakh transactions — driven by Diwali festive season consumer spending surge.
2. India processes ₹12.8 lakh crores worth of UPI transactions every month on average — reflecting the extraordinary scale of digital payment adoption in under a decade.
3. 2024 recorded the highest ATM activity with all 12 months exceeding 350 lakh withdrawals — cash usage remains persistently high despite UPI growth, confirming digital payments are additive not substitutive.
4. NEFT dominates total transaction value at ₹212.5 lakh crores vs UPI's ₹93.7 lakh crores — UPI leads in volume frequency while NEFT remains the backbone for high-value institutional transfers.
5. Clear inverse relationship between UPI value and ATM withdrawal value — as UPI grew, cash withdrawal amounts declined but visit frequency remained high, meaning Indians withdraw smaller amounts per ATM visit.
6. October consistently records peak credit card spending — Diwali festive season drives simultaneous spikes across all payment modes.
7. Credit cards outpaced debit cards by ₹98,826 crores over 6 years — Indians are increasingly shifting toward credit-based spending.
8. UPI transaction volume exceeds NEFT by 23x in recent months — UPI has completely dominated retail payment frequency.
9. India's UPI journey shows three clear phases — Low adoption (2019–2020), Medium (2021–2022), High (2023–present).
10. 34 of 73 months exceeded average UPI value — all from 2023 onwards, confirming a structural shift in digital payment scale post-2023.
